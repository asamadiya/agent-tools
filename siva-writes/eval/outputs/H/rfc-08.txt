- **Restricted state transition:** The `schedulingGates` field can be initialized only when a Pod is created (either by the client, or mutated during admission). After creation, each `schedulingGate` can be removed in arbitrary order, but addition of a new scheduling gate is disallowed. To ensure consistency, scheduled Pod must always have empty `schedulingGates`. This means that a client (an administrator or custom controller) cannot create a Pod that has `schedulingGates` populated in any way, if that Pod also specified a `spec.nodeName`.
