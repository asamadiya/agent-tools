Cost programme codenamed Trim. Owner Renee Alvarez, finance partner Sanjay Kothari, monthly reporting cadence. Target is $28M annualised infrastructure savings by end of FY27. Booked to date is $16.4M, 59% of target. Composition of the booked amount: $9.1M from right-sizing 6,800 over-provisioned pods, median CPU request cut from 4.0 cores to 1.5; $4.2M from deleting 41 PB of object storage untouched for more than three years; $3.1M from converting 62% of the steady-state fleet to reserved instances. The remaining $11.6M is planned as $7.0M from GPU bin-packing, where cluster utilization is 38% today against a 65% target, and $4.6M from decommissioning three legacy datacenter halls by June 2027. Right-sizing caused five incidents, four SEV-3 and one SEV-2, all CPU throttling on services whose p99 CPU sat close to the new request. A guardrail shipped Aug 20 now blocks any cut where measured p99 CPU exceeds 70% of the proposed request; it has rejected 410 of 1,100 candidate cuts since.
