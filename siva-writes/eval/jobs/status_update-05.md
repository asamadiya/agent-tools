Experiment two-tower-v7, a session-aware retrieval head added to notifications ranking. Owner Aditi Sundaram. The LIX ramped to 2% on Jul 14, 4% on Aug 4, 10% on Aug 19, and was shut down Sep 5. Headline results at 10%: notification CTR -0.11% with a 95% confidence interval of -0.41% to +0.19%, so indistinguishable from zero. Downstream sessions per member -0.6% at p = 0.003, a significant regression. Serving cost up 31%, 4,100 GPU-hours per day against 3,130 for control. p99 scoring latency moved from 38 ms to 57 ms. Segment cut: members in the top decile by weekly session count gained +1.9% CTR, while the 62% of members with fewer than three sessions a week lost 0.8%, and that segment dominates the aggregate. Working hypothesis for the miss is that the session head needs at least four prior sessions to produce a useful embedding and falls back to a near-constant vector otherwise, which flattens ranking for light users. Decision taken Sep 5 with the metrics council: do not ramp, tear down the LIX, but keep the session embedding table in the feature store because two other teams now read it.
